# What changed in UK tax this year

Every UK tax figure that moved between two tax years — and every one that was
frozen, which is usually the more interesting half.

**Live:** <https://dev-lab-x.github.io/uk-tax-year-changes/>

Generated by diffing two files from the
[uk-tax-rates](https://github.com/HadiDevLabx/uk-tax-rates) dataset. No figure
on the page is typed by hand.

## Why generated, not written

The first version of this diff matched student loan plans **by array index**
and reported a Postgraduate Loan change from £21,000/6% to £25,000/9% that
never happened — Plan 5 had been inserted ahead of it in 2026/27, shifting
every later element by one. Plans are matched by key now, and bands by label.

That is the whole argument for generating a page like this rather than writing
it: the error was invisible in prose and obvious in a diff.

## 2026/27 vs 2025/26

**Moved:** Scottish starter and basic rate ceilings, Plan 1, Plan 2 and Plan 4
thresholds, and Plan 5 arriving as a new plan at £25,000 / 9%.

**Frozen:** the personal allowance, the £100,000 taper, England and Wales
income tax bands, and employee National Insurance.

A frozen threshold is a tax rise nobody has to announce.
